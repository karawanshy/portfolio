# Recruiter Review

Use to evaluate the Portfolio from the perspective of a recruiter performing a fast scan.

## Process

1. Open the rendered Portfolio without first inspecting its source.
2. Review the initial desktop viewport as a new visitor.
3. Follow the natural scan/scroll path for roughly 30 seconds.
4. Repeat the initial scan on mobile.
5. Determine whether these are quickly understandable:
   - identity;
   - current role;
   - engineering focus and relevant skills;
   - standout work/impact;
   - contact or next-step links.
6. Note important information that exists but is visually difficult to discover.
7. Classify findings as BLOCKER, IMPORTANT, or POLISH.

## Principles

- Judge what a visitor can actually perceive, not what exists in the DOM.
- Creativity is not a problem unless it interferes with comprehension.
- Do not require every detail above the fold.
- The first viewport should establish identity and professional direction and provide an obvious path forward.
- Standout work and deeper technical information may appear naturally during the first short scroll.
- Evaluate desktop and mobile separately when their experience differs.
